Each species uses its own base hunger lifetime. Restore chance is converted to a check each second and can activate more than once per minute. A Restore pet cannot activate its ability at zero hunger, but an active Restore pet can target and revive a pet at zero. Each restore gives its full share of the target's bar, capped only by the room left in it. Restores per hour and uptime are averages while the listed Restore sources remain active, so unlucky streaks can perform worse.
